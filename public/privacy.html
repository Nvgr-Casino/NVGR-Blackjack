Last updated 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information NVGR Casino ("we", "us") collects when you use the NVGR Casino app and website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

Other players can see your display name, profile picture, character, level, join date, game statistics and whether you are online or when you were last seen. Players at your table see your seat, bets and table chat. Private messages are visible only to you and the person you are writing to.

We use Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) to sign you in and store game data, and Google Sign-In if you choose it.
